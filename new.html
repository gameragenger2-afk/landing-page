IN THE AGE OF AI AND ROBOTS, WE STILL BELIEVE IN HUMANS

Every time you call, you get a real person. Not an algorithm. Not a voice recognition system. Not a chatbot pretending to be human. Meet our specialists.

WHY HUMAN VERIFICATION MATTERS:
Artificial Intelligence has made scams more convincing than ever. AI can clone voices, write convincing emails, and fool automated systems. Only a trained human specialist can ask the right questions, hear the fear in your voice, and provide the reassurance that no machine can offer. Read about AI voice cloning threats.

HOW OUR SERVICE WORKS

The Care Givers Plan Digital Protection Initiative provides members with immediate telephone access to certified Senior Protection Specialists. When you encounter suspicious calls, emails, or visitors, one button connects you to a human expert who will:

  • Listen to your concerns with patience and respect
  • Verify suspicious callers while you wait on the line
  • Contact your family members to alert them of potential fraud attempts
  • Stay connected with you until you feel completely safe
  • Provide clear, jargon-free guidance you can trust
